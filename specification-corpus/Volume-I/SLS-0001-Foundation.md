# SLS-0001 — Foundation

Status: Accepted  
Version: 1.0  
Authority: Spider Labs Architecture  
Date: 2026-07-06

## Purpose

This specification defines the philosophical and engineering foundation of Spider Labs and Compound.

Implementation details may evolve. These principles do not.

## Mission

Spider Labs builds software that earns trust through refinement.

## Vision

Create software that improves human work while preserving human ownership.

## Product philosophy

Spider Labs does not replace creators.

Spider Labs refines their work.

The human remains the author. The software remains the instrument.

## Engineering philosophy

Software should be:

- explainable
- inspectable
- repeatable
- deterministic where possible
- modular
- portable

Software should not be:

- magical
- opaque
- destructive
- vendor-dependent
- hostile to its user's ownership

## Ten-Year Test

Every significant decision must answer:

> Would this still make sense if Spider Labs exists in ten years?

If the answer is no, redesign the solution.

## Success definition

Spider Labs succeeds when users trust its recommendations without surrendering ownership of their work.

## Operating sentence

Build software that earns trust through refinement, not automation.
