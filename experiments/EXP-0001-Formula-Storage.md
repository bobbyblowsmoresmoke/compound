# EXP-0001 Formula Storage

Status: Open

## Question

Should Formula files be stored as TOML plus Markdown, YAML, JSON, or another portable format?

## Hypothesis

TOML metadata plus Markdown body gives the best balance of machine validation and human readability.

## Method

Implement small sample Formula files in candidate formats and compare readability, validation, and compatibility with Qwen-Code implementation.

## Decision

Pending.
