# SLS-0017 Anti-Goals

Status: Accepted
Version: 1.0
Depends On: SLS-0010

## Purpose

This specification defines what Compound refuses to become.

## Anti-goals

Compound is not a chatbot.

Compound is not an autonomous marketplace bot.

Compound is not SaaS-first.

Compound is not dependent on a single model vendor.

Compound is not opaque AI magic.

Compound is not destructive.

Compound is not a browser automation platform in v0.1.

Compound is not a dashboard-first analytics product.

## Rule

Any feature pushing Compound toward an anti-goal must be deferred or redesigned.
