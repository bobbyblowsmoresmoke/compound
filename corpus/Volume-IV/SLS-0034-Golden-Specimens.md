# SLS-0034 Golden Specimens

Status: Draft
Version: 0.1

## Purpose

Golden Specimens are stable fixtures used to detect quality regressions.

## Initial categories

Fashion.
Electronics.
Books.
Collectibles.
Automotive.
Household.

## Rules

Golden Specimens are regression instruments.

A change that degrades deterministic Assay results must be explained before merge.

## Required data

Each Golden Specimen should include raw input, normalized Specimen, expected Assay findings, and expected output shape.
