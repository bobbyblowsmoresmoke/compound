# SLS-0005 Product Principles

Status: Accepted
Version: 1.0
Depends On: SLS-0001, SLS-0002, SLS-0003, SLS-0004

## Purpose

This specification defines product invariants for Compound.

## Principles

### PP-001 Artifact refinement

Compound is an artifact refinement engine.

### PP-002 Listing-first

Listings are the first artifact, not the only artifact.

### PP-003 Pipeline-first

Every artifact passes through the Compound Pipeline.

### PP-004 Revisions, not replacements

Compound produces revisions, not destructive overwrites.

### PP-005 Quality through Assays

Quality is measured through Assays, not intuition.

### PP-006 AI as Catalyst

AI may execute refinement. AI does not define correctness.

### PP-007 Local ownership

Compound must preserve the user's local ownership of artifacts, Formulas, outputs, and Notebook records.

### PP-008 Inspectable output

Compound outputs must be inspectable before acceptance or use.
