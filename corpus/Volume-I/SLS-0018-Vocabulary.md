# SLS-0018 Vocabulary

Status: Accepted
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This specification freezes core vocabulary for Compound.

## Canonical terms

Specimen means immutable artifact under refinement.

Formula means versioned refinement protocol.

Catalyst means interchangeable execution adapter.

Assay means deterministic evaluation.

Finding means structured diagnostic output.

Reagent means external adapter or exporter.

Distillation means generated output artifact.

Lab Notebook means permanent experiment history.

Experiment means a structured research question.

Corpus means the authoritative specification set.

## Rule

Do not introduce synonyms for canonical terms in specifications or implementation names unless the Corpus explicitly authorizes them.
