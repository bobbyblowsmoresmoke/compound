# SLS-0052 Marketplace Domains

Status: Draft
Version: 0.1

## Purpose

This specification defines how marketplaces are modeled.

## Rule

Marketplaces are external domains represented through Formulas, Assays, and Reagents.

The core does not know about marketplace APIs or browser flows.

## Initial marketplace targets

eBay-style output may be used as a Formula target.

No autonomous posting is authorized in v0.1.
